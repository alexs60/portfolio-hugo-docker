When a link that should open an app opens the browser instead, the cause is usually one of two small files on the website: `apple-app-site-association` for iOS or `assetlinks.json` for Android. Deep Link Doctor fetches both, checks them against what Apple and Google expect, and explains in plain words what is wrong.

Free, with no login.
