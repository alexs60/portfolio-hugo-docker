From February 2027, batteries sold in the EU need a Digital Product Passport: a record of what the battery is made of, where it comes from and its carbon footprint, reachable from a QR code. Textiles come next. Most of the market sells this as an enterprise integration project. TraceApi is for small importers who need to comply without one.

The core is a Go API, with web apps for importers and manufacturers. Early access is by email through the site.
