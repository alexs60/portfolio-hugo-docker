Students practise by topic and take mock exams that work like the real theory test: 30 questions, 20 minutes, three mistakes allowed. A light turns green after five passes in a row, and the school sees the same light, so instructors know who is ready to book the exam.

Free for students. Driving schools pay from €39 a month after a 30-day trial. In Italian and English.
